What does Shift do in your game?

Thresholds

Move Pedal faster than this to move.
Start sprint Pedal faster than this to start sprinting.
Stop sprint Once sprinting, slow below this to stop sprinting.

Quick stop

Without this you keep moving while the pedals spin down. The shaded areas on the live speed graph show when quick stop kicked in.

Drop needed (%) How far the speed must drop below the slowest point of your last pedal stroke to count as stopping. Lower stops sooner. Raise it if you stop by mistake while still pedalling.
Counter-strafe (ms) Briefly presses the opposite movement key when you stop, so you stop dead instead of sliding. 0 = off.

Changes apply straight away. Save keeps them after the Arduino is unplugged.
